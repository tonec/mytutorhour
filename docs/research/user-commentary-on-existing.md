We use Teachworks at our business. We started a trial with Tutorbird, but at the time there were a few quirky things with importing our students and a few features that could not be turned off, so opted for TW.

Teachworks has been impressive and covers almost all of our needs. Some of the features, like scheduling, are completely intuitive, but there are some other aspects that really are not intuitive and require some digging to really figure them out. That being said, once figured out, they are pretty powerful. Customer support has always been excellent when you have a question.

Big pros are the ease of calendar and the integration of stripe with auto invoices and auto pay. Once these are set up (which is not one of the intuitive things), it is a set it and forget it thing, which has been such a time saver.

The biggest swing and miss I see is the inability for a client to see a clean statement of transactions. Every lesson (or multiple lessons) is "invoiced" and then payments are made against those invoices, and honestly it's very clunky for clients. So if clients look at their list of transactions, they see an invoice for a certain amount, but then have to go in and see what days those lessons were specifically on. It's not intuitive for the client. It would be much easier if there was some sort of statement that could be created that showed all lessons in a month and all payments made.

But that one glaring downside does not outweigh the pros, and I would recommend it. Happy to answer any questions you have about it.
